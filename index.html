Our Farm

From the soil of Rajasthan

Rajasthan's dry climate and mineral-rich soil are ideal for spices like jeera, saunf and methi. For years our family has grown these crops on our own land.

Now we are bringing them directly to your kitchen. There are no middlemen, no mixing and no shortcuts, just spices as they should be.
